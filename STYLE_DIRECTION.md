# AFTER 02:17 — Chapter 1 v1.1 — Visual direction

## Reference principle

The room uses the broad visual grammar of early abandoned-toy-factory horror:
- light, once-cheerful interior surfaces that have aged badly;
- primary-color accents (red / yellow / turquoise) used to guide the eye;
- square tiled floor with occasional colored tiles;
- industrial service hardware breaking through the playful palette;
- darkness and weak fluorescent lighting creating the horror contrast.

No Poppy Playtime logos, characters, murals, exact room layouts or branded props are copied.

## Room identity

This is a **night security / employee break room**. Objects are grouped by purpose rather than scattered:
- north wall: exit, clock, maintenance locker;
- east wall: power and emergency-access hardware;
- south-west: security workstation;
- west: archive shelf and manual hidden-compartment mechanism;
- south-east: old waiting sofa, rug, floor lamp;
- north-east: maintenance storage boxes.

The central path remains walkable so the player can understand the room quickly and the final chase has a readable route.

## Material logic

Each visible 3D surface category has a local photographic CC0 texture:
ceramic tiles, plaster, painted metal, rusted metal, wood, leather, cloth, carpet, cardboard and rubber/dark plastic.
Colored floor accents still use the ceramic tile texture (tinted), not a mismatched metal material.
